# Independent cloud fall performance assessment

Read-only source/profile assessment by the independent RTLS reviewer. No kernel
edits, benchmark, profile execution or acceptance changes. The lead explicitly
reports that the owner moved acceptance to cloud; this assessment uses the
recorded cloud baseline and does not substitute historical Mac timing.

## Verdict

Do not spend the remaining bounded attempt on an unsubstantiated representation
rewrite. The required capped reduction is 48.61% (3.89177782 ms to 2 ms). Existing
evidence establishes no exact-output candidate with that magnitude. Recommend
an explicitly declared, independently reviewed JIT/representation diagnosis as
the next bounded approach, keeping the production kernel frozen until its
mechanism is established. Standing reviewed-cycle authorization allows a fresh
cycle if the current cycle is exhausted; it does not allow luck reruns.

## Source and raw-profile findings

The raw cpuprofile contains only nodes, samples, timeDeltas and start/end time;
node data has callFrame, children, hitCount and positionTicks. It contains no
optimization tier, deoptimization reason, inline decision, IC state or object
map evidence. Separate visible function nodes therefore do not prove either
failed inlining or expensive polymorphic shapes. Inclusive attribution is
mixed normal/capped/warm-up work and cannot quantify a capped-only speedup.

advanceUnpoweredFall already keeps integration coordinates, velocities, step
count and stable parameters in local scalars, writing continuation fields at
the end of a slice. UnpoweredFallWork and BurnScratch have fixed-field factories;
the inspected hot path adds/deletes no fields. Damage metadata and zero-command
area are already prepared, with finite-force and invalid-angle validation
retained. There is no source-demonstrated hidden-class churn to eliminate.

fallAcceleration does stage intermediate drag/lift/motion/attack values through
scratch.inputs and then call writeAccelerationComponents. A scalar argument or
fused representation is a plausible future compiler investigation, but the
visible composition subtree is only about 5.30% of the fall tree. The kernel's
49.12% self attribution includes intrinsic math and potentially inlined helpers;
it is not removable object-access cost. Eliminating all of that self cost would
barely reach the required reduction and is impossible while keeping the force
law. ISA (~11.40%) and cross-section (~4.70%) likewise do not provide a credible
48.61% plan. Overlapping inclusive percentages must not be added.

The composed helper already shares motion sine/cosine and lift-sign queries.
Preparing zero thrust repeats a historically rejected approach. Geometric memo
is invalid: pitch remains 30 degrees and the capped output has 3246.2139561608574
m lateral displacement. Motion, attack, lift and cross-section evolve. Replacing
trig by algebraic identities, regrouping drag/lift products, removing gravity's
subtract/add sequence, or dropping zero-thrust products is not an exact-output
representation change.

## Smallest evidence-producing approach

After the current frame baseline releases the exclusive CPU slot, declare one
source-pinned unchanged-kernel JIT diagnostic using the supported Node22 runtime
with optimization/deoptimization and inlining traces. Keep exactly the recorded
workloads, warm counts, exception behavior and 4000 cap. Do not claim its timings
as acceptance. Inspect actual optimization/deoptimization reasons and generated
representation before choosing a code change; retain the raw logs and a concise
mapped summary. An unavailable/unsupported trace is a diagnostic failure, not
permission to change runtime flags or weaken budgets.

If concrete evidence identifies a call-boundary or object-representation issue,
the narrow candidate is an allocation-free scalar/local fall-force composition
that preserves every original operation, order and scratch side effect. It must
also preserve caller-mutated gimbal, pitch, fixed/gimballed thrust and nonfinite
inputs, not assume scratch remains equal to its factory defaults. Keep both
midpoint force evaluations, ISA evaluations, damage validation and cap/slice
semantics. Preserve the independent original same-runtime oracle, dense cases,
interleaving, exceptions, signed zero and scratch mutation proofs. Only after
exact proof and independent review may one declared unprofiled acceptance check
measure the candidate. A measured failure is retained and requires a new
reviewed approach, not another unchanged run.
